L&T Infotech Evolution

From infrastructure roots to digital transformation leader

1996

Foundation

L&T Information Technology Ltd founded in December as a subsidiary of Larsen & Toubro, marking the beginning of L&T's IT services journey.

Foundation L&T Subsidiary
2001-2002

Rebranding & Quality Excellence

Company renamed to L&T Infotech Ltd. and achieved SEI Maturity Level 5, establishing quality processes and standards.

Rebranding SEI Level 5 Quality
2009-2012

Growth Strategy

Larsen & Toubro initiated scaling efforts through acquisition attempts for major IT companies including Satyam, Patni, and Hexaware.

Expansion M&A Strategy
2013-2014

Focus Refinement

Engineering services segment transferred to L&T Technology Services, allowing LTI to focus on core IT services and digital solutions.

Restructuring Focus
2016

Public Listing

L&T Infotech went public with an IPO of ₹1,243 crore, marking a new phase of growth and market recognition.

IPO Public Company ₹1,243 Cr
2017

Brand Evolution

Rebranded as 'LTI' with the tagline 'Let's Solve', reflecting the company's problem-solving approach and modern identity.

Rebranding LTI Let's Solve
2017-2021

Strategic Acquisitions

Multiple strategic acquisitions including Syncordis (banking), Nielsen+Partner (consulting), Lymbyc (AI/ML), and Cuelogic (digital engineering).

Acquisitions Banking Tech AI/ML Digital Engineering

Mindtree Evolution

From startup to billion-dollar IT services company

1999

The Beginning

Mindtree Consulting Private Limited founded by ten IT professionals in August, with initial funding from Walden International and Sivan Securities.

Startup 10 Founders VC Funded
2001

Series B Funding

Additional funding received from Capital Group and Franklin Templeton, providing growth capital for expansion.

Series B Growth Capital
2007

Public Debut

IPO launched in February, oversubscribed by 100+ times, demonstrating strong market confidence in the company's growth potential.

IPO 100x Oversubscribed BSE/NSE
2012

Global Expansion

New brand identity with "Welcome to possible" slogan launched. First US delivery center established in Gainesville, Florida.

Rebranding US Expansion Welcome to Possible
2004-2016

Strategic Acquisitions

Series of acquisitions including Aztecsoft (2008), Bluefin Solutions (2015), and Magnet 360 (2016) to expand capabilities and market reach.

M&A Growth SAP/ERP Salesforce UK Market
2017

Global Presence

Expanded to 43 offices across 17 countries, establishing strong international footprint and client proximity.

43 Offices 17 Countries Global Reach
2019

Billion Dollar Milestone

Achieved $1 billion in annual revenue, joining the exclusive billion-dollar IT services companies club in India.

$1B Revenue Growth Milestone Market Leader

The Merger Story

Creating India's 5th largest IT services company

June 2019

Strategic Acquisition

Larsen & Toubro completed acquisition of Mindtree, marking the first acquisition in Indian IT industry, despite opposition from Mindtree's founders.

Acquisition First in IT 61% Stake
May 2022

Merger Announcement

L&T announced the merger of Mindtree with LTI to create a combined entity, leveraging complementary strengths and capabilities.

Merger Announced Strategic Synergy
November 2022

LTIMindtree Born

Merger completed successfully. LTIMindtree became India's 5th largest IT services company by market cap and 6th by revenue.

Merger Complete 5th Largest Market Cap Leader

Synergy Realization
